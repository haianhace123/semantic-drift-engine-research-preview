Who is responsible for your data

Semantic Drift Engine is self-hosted software, not a centrally operated cloud service. The organization operating this instance determines the purposes and means of its processing, subject to its own contracts and applicable law. Before admitting users, the operator must publish its legal identity, address or appropriate contact channel, effective date, privacy contact and any required processor information. The open-source maintainers are not automatically the operator of your instance.

Information the system may process

Account and security data may include names, email addresses, organization memberships, password hashes, sessions, API-key records, consent records, security events and service activity. Monitored knowledge may include source URLs, source contents, snapshots, revision identifiers, embeddings, semantic assessments, lineage observations, downstream target identities, alerts, audit records and operator actions. Depending on the material submitted, these records can contain personal or confidential information.

Sources and purposes

Information may come from users, organization administrators, monitored sources, connected applications and runtime infrastructure. It is used for authentication, authorization, knowledge monitoring, change analysis, lineage-based impact assessment, operations, security, troubleshooting and audit evidence. Each deployment operator must establish and disclose the lawful basis for each applicable activity, including any consent or other conditions required by local law.

Storage, retention and backups

The deployment may use PostgreSQL, Redis and local snapshot storage. Some security and execution records are retained for auditability. Account deactivation does not remove all related records. There is no single verified retention period across the software, its backups, downstream systems and optional providers. The operator must determine and publish retention periods or objective criteria, deletion procedures and backup retention before the deployment accepts personal data.

Service providers and international transfers

Depending on configuration, data may be sent to external embedding or language-model providers, a transactional email provider, webhook destinations or separately managed downstream databases. These services may process information in other jurisdictions. The operator must identify the providers used in its deployment, document data categories transferred, establish any required agreements and assess applicable international transfer rules. Providers that are not configured need not receive application data.

Cookies and authentication

The application uses authentication and session cookies and a CSRF protection mechanism. These support sign-in, session renewal and protection of account actions. Operators should separately disclose any additional analytics or tracking introduced by their hosting environment; this source-code template does not attest to such additions.

Security

The software includes authentication, permission checks, tenant-scoped interfaces and controls for selected secrets and operations. No software control eliminates every risk. Secure configuration, network boundaries, encryption in transit, backup protection, credential rotation, incident response and independent testing remain deployment responsibilities. Do not submit secrets or sensitive source data to an instance whose operator and safeguards you do not trust.

Access, correction, export and deletion

Where enabled, a user may access account information, export profile and consent records, and request account deactivation. The available account export is not an export of organization knowledge. Deactivation can be blocked for the last active administrator until an authorized succession or closure process is completed. It does not erase monitored sources, snapshots, vectors, lineage, security history, backups or provider copies. Requests concerning these records, or other rights provided by applicable law, require separate review by the deployment operator.

Children and restricted information

The project is intended for authorized organizational use, not a service specifically directed to children. Operators must assess age restrictions and the treatment of sensitive or regulated data before enabling the collection of such material. The repository's publication does not certify any deployment for a regulated use case.

Changes and contact

The deployment operator must publish an effective date, explain how material policy changes are communicated and provide a functioning privacy contact for questions and rights requests. No operator contact or retention schedule has been established by this template. It must not be presented as a completed notice for public onboarding.
